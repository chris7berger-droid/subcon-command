SESSION SUMMARY
First read-only office Time Clock slice for Friday's field test. The screen
finds recorded punches by date range, employee, job, and customer, and shows
stored regular/OT hours. Preview is Ready. No merge and no production release.

CHANGES SHIPPED (PREVIEW ONLY)
/field/timeclock reads time_punches directly for an inclusive punch_date range.
Both dates are required; invalid or reversed ranges are rejected. The default
is today in America/Los_Angeles. The query does not start from active Schedule
jobs. call_log (job number, name, customer) and team_members (employee name)
are left-resolved. Missing related rows stay in the list, labeled with the raw
id or a missing-value label. Employee, job, and customer filters use ids, so
duplicate names stay separate. Customer name is a label only. Unmatched punches
remain when no customer is selected.

Columns: work date, punch date (Pacific), punch time (Pacific), employee, job,
customer, punch type, regular hours, OT hours. Unfamiliar punch types render.
Regular and OT show the stored value: real zero stays visible, null stays
blank, including on non-clock-out rows. No duration, lunch, overtime, or
paid-hours calculation, and no hours total.

Pages are ordered by punch_date, punch_time, then punch id. A failed page
throws and is not shown as a complete result. Punch id is the row key. A newer
range clears the previous rows and ignores an older response.

fetchTodayRows, fetchActiveFieldJobs, shared fetchAll, and shared useAsync are
unchanged. The old fetchFieldPunches helper remains in queries.js and is no
longer used by this screen.

DECISIONS
Office visibility of stored regular/OT is in this slice. The phone hours writer
was not changed. Blank hours are a data gap, not a calculated substitute.
Queued and not built: work-type reporting, quick audited corrections,
Live/Issues views, configurable CSV exports.
