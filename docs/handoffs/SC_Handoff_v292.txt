WHAT THE SCREEN DOES
Quick columns: Employee, Shift date, Job number, Job name, Customer,
Clock in, Lunch out, Lunch in, Clock out, Regular hours, Overtime hours,
Work hours, Status, Actions. Shift dates read “Sep 21”. Punch times are
Pacific with AM/PM. A punch on another calendar day is marked with that
day. Employee stays pinned while the table scrolls. Job and customer
names wrap. Regular, overtime, and work hours stay on the shift that
earned them.

Expanded adds drive start, drive end, drive hours, and the full Pacific
date and time on each punch. Lunch in stays blank unless a lunch-in punch
was recorded. The 30-minute deduction does not invent a timestamp.

Two open clock-ins stay two rows. The status is “Overlapping clock-ins”
and the detail names both jobs and times. Review punches opens those
records. A historical open shift stays “Missing clock-out”. “In progress”
is only a shift whose Pacific start day is today. Hours math is unchanged.

Add, Edit, and Void: Review changes, then Confirm add, Confirm changes,
or Confirm void. The summary shows employee, job, punch, full Pacific
time, and reason. An edit shows original → proposed. Back returns to the
draft. Cancel writes nothing. Confirm submits the reviewed values once
and disables while saving. A stale save says to reload and review again
and does not resubmit.
