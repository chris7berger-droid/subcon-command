IMPLEMENTED
Regular hours are the first 40 actual worked hours in the Monday–Sunday
week that contains the shift’s Pacific start day. Overtime is the worked
time above 40. Driving is paid and counts toward the 40, once. The drive
column stays a breakdown and is not added again. A closed shift with a
lunch start still deducts exactly 30 unpaid minutes. A late or missing
lunch end does not change that deduction.

Classification uses the employee’s whole week and every job before the
date, job, or customer filter. A Friday-only view of a week that already
has 40 hours shows the extra hours as overtime. Filtering to one job does
not reclassify those hours.

An incomplete or ambiguous record keeps a specific explanation, such as
“Missing clock-out”, “Clock-out without a clock-in”, or “Lunch is longer
than the shift”. If any record in that employee-week cannot be classified,
regular and overtime stay blank on the whole week and a complete row says
“Week has a missing punch”. Missing hours are not treated as zero.

Holiday benefit hours are a separate column and stay blank. No holiday
calendar or eligibility list exists, and that does not block ordinary
weekly regular and overtime. No holiday punches are invented, and no
extra double-time multiplier is applied.

The employee dropdown lists active team members, including people with no
punches in the selected range, plus anyone who already has a punch in the
loaded week. Job number and job name are separate. A job name that repeats
the number is shown once. The editor asks for employee, job, punch, date,
time, and a reason. Save and void stop at “Saving is unavailable.”
